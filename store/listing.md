## Before you submit

  the app hasn't been run end to end yet. Store policy requires the listing to "accurately and
  clearly reflect" the product's features, so delete any line for a feature that doesn't work
  in the build you submit.
- **No donation button in the Store build.** Store Policy 10.8.2 says voluntary donations
  must use Microsoft's payment request API or a secure third-party purchase API. Whether a
  link that opens a donation page in the browser counts is unclear, so Ocula hides its
  "Support Ocula" button when installed from the Store (`AppInfo.DonationsInStoreBuild = false`)
  and this listing doesn't mention donations. Keep the donation link on your GitHub page. If
  you later want it in the Store build, check the current policy first, then flip that switch.
- **Don't put other companies' brand names in the search terms,** and keep them to words that
  genuinely describe Ocula. The policy forbids irrelevant terms.

## Description (3,067 of 10,000 characters)

Plain text. The Store shows line breaks and the • bullets as typed.

```
Ocula is a fast, modern photo browser for Windows. See your folders, a grid of thumbnails and a large preview side by side, in light or dark mode, and get through a shoot without opening a heavy editor.

OPENS WHAT YOU SHOOT
• RAW files from most cameras (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2, DNG and more), plus JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, AVIF and WebP
• RAW+JPEG pairs appear as one photo
• Video too: MP4, MOV and WMV play with Windows' own player, and MKV, WebM, AVI and others play with a built-in engine that adds audio-track and subtitle menus
• Browse photos on a NAS or in OneDrive without downloading online-only files

FIND THE KEEPERS FAST
• Sharpness scores flag soft shots, and one click hides them
• Bursts collapse into stacks
• Compare two or four photos side by side with linked zoom, then pick or reject
• Star ratings and colour labels with Lightroom-style keyboard shortcuts
• The duplicate finder spots exact copies and near-identical shots, and sends the extras to the Recycle Bin

STAY ORGANISED
• Tags, ratings and labels are saved in your files or in standard .xmp sidecar files, so other photo apps can read them. RAW files are never modified
• Search your whole library with plain words or filters, for example: rating>=4 tag:wedding year:2026
• Timeline, collections and smart albums organise photos without moving or copying files
• A map shows every photo that has a GPS location
• Ocula remembers thumbnails and details, so big folders and NAS folders reopen instantly

GET PHOTOS IN AND OUT
• Import from memory cards into dated folders, with renaming, tags and a backup copy to a second folder. Photos already imported are skipped, and the card can be ejected when done
• Batch rename with undo
• Export with presets: resize, convert (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF), remove location data and add a watermark
• PDF contact sheets
• Client proofing galleries: a web page your clients use to heart their favourites, with no accounts or servers. Paste their list back into Ocula to mark those photos as picked
• Send photos to your editor of choice with one click

SEE YOUR PHOTOS PROPERLY
• True HDR preview on HDR displays, for Ultra HDR and other gain-map JPEGs and for PQ and HLG images
• Accurate colour for Display P3 and Adobe RGB photos
• Full-screen viewer with smooth zoom and a filmstrip
• Slideshows, and a second-screen mode for showing photos to a client on a TV or projector
• Quick edits: crop, straighten, rotate, exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, warmth and saturation, saved as a copy by default so your originals stay untouched

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your photos stay on your PC. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. A few optional features use the internet, and each has an off switch in Settings: camera photos (only the camera's model name is sent), the map (it loads map tiles), daily update checks, and downloading images from web pages you choose.

FREE
Ocula is free to use.

TIPS
For the fastest RAW thumbnails, install Microsoft's free Raw Image Extension from the Store. To see HDR photos in HDR, turn on "Use HDR" in Windows display settings.
```

## Additional system requirements

Minimum hardware:

- Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit (x64)
- 4 GB of memory
- Disk space for the app and its library of thumbnails, which grows with your photo collection

Recommended hardware:

- Windows 11
- 8 GB of memory or more for large photo libraries
- An HDR-capable display with Use HDR turned on, to see HDR photos in HDR
- Microsoft's free Raw Image Extension, for fast RAW thumbnails
- Microsoft's free HEIF and AV1 extensions, to open HEIC and AVIF photos

Set the minimum Windows version here to match the one in `Package.appxmanifest`.
