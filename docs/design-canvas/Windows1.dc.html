The Snap tab
One click, any spotEvery photo window has a Snap tab in the title bar. Pick a layout, then click the zone: far left, middle right, far right and so on. The window moves and resizes to fit.
Works on any monitorIt lists each display. “Send to Display 2” moves the window to the other screen and keeps its zone.
Keyboard tooAlt+1 to Alt+4 place the window in that column. Alt+0 centres it. No mouse needed.
RemembersTurn on “Remember this spot” and the next photo you open lands there.
